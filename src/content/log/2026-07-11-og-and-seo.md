---
title: Social cards and the SEO pass
date: 2026-07-11
by: Roy Bos, reviewed by Jasmine Pekoe
---

Every page now generates its own Open Graph image at build time, drawn with
Satori in the studio's own colours, so a shared link shows a proper card instead
of a bare URL. The card is composed as HTML and rasterised to PNG during the
build, which leaves the edge doing nothing but serving a static image.

The same pass finished the metadata. Full Open Graph and Twitter tags, unique
per-page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, a robots file, and structured data
on the home page. Small, unglamorous work, and it is the difference between a
link that looks like it came from somewhere real and one that does not.
